Frequently asked questions

Does Pro include the Free Edition?

Pro declares the Free Edition as a dependency. When both packages are available in the addons path, Odoo installs the dependency automatically; validating Free first is recommended.

Is US$49 a subscription?

No. It is a one-time app price for the purchased Odoo major version. Services and infrastructure are separate.

Can cleanup cause data loss?

Cleanup requires retention eligibility, a stored SHA-256, and fresh checksum verification of both the local copy and live S3 object. Any missing or mismatched proof preserves the local copy.

Which providers are supported?

AWS S3 and MinIO are validated. Other S3-compatible providers can be checked with Test Connection.

Who is Pro designed for?

It fits Odoo.sh and self-hosted teams that need safe automated routing, cleanup, and restore. Provider-to-provider migration, throttling, and monetary cost forecasting are outside the current scope.

How do I request support?

Use the publisher support contact with your Odoo and module versions, reproduction steps, and relevant logs. Infrastructure, migration, and customization are separate services.
